# Study Notebooks — Interview Script
### One Student. Three Steps. One Sitting. · 15 pts per verified participant · min 10, max 50

Each interview: **15–20 minutes**. A participant counts as **verified** only when **both** the pre-survey and the post-survey are filled in — by them, not by you.

---

## Before your first interview

- [ ] Pre-survey link + QR ready on your phone
- [ ] **Post-survey link ready — it's a different form**
- [ ] One notebook pre-built with your own course material (not a generic topic)
- [ ] All three demos run end to end, once, by you
- [ ] Quiet spot, or a booked quiet online slot
- [ ] A list of **13 names**, not 10 — people drop out
- [ ] Anyone who already knows Study Notebooks well is off the list

---

## The three steps

| Step | What it does | What you show |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| **1. Create a Notebook** | Grounds everything in *their* material | Upload their real PPTs / notes |
| **2. Diagnostic Quiz** | Finds the gaps | Their weak units appearing for the first time |
| **3. Progress Dashboard** | Tells them what to study next | Strengths, focus areas, priorities |

Step 2 is the one that changes faces. Let it land — don't talk over it.

---

## Opening

> Use this as a guide, not a script. Talk naturally.

```
YOU: "Hey! I'm [Name], Google Student Ambassador for [Campus]. Got a few
minutes? I want to show you something that helps you build full-fledged notes
for your exams, without the hours it usually takes. Got 10 minutes?"

(wait for yes)

YOU: "Perfect — before we start, can you fill this quick form? Scan the QR here."
[share pre-survey link — WAIT until they finish]

YOU: "Okay, where are you with exam prep right now? Be honest — on track,
a bit behind, or haven't opened the books yet?"
```

⚠️ The pre-survey goes **before** you start talking. Hand it over, then wait in silence.

---

## Q1 – Q4

Q1 and Q2 surface the pain. Q3 sums up what they need. Q4 introduces the answer.

```
Q1  "When your professor shares the syllabus and a pile of PPTs and PDFs,
     how do you decide what to study first?"

Q2  "How do you make notes right now — handwritten, typed, borrowing a friend's,
     screenshotting slides? Which part do you hate the most?"

Q3  "If a tool used your own class material to find your weak units and taught
     those first, would you use it?"

Q4  "That's close to what Study Notebooks does inside Google Gemini. It works
     from your own PPTs and notes. Let me show you."
```

---

## Demo 1 — Create a Notebook

First ask **what material they actually have** for their next exam. Then use exactly that.

```
"First is Create a Notebook. Go to Google Gemini in your browser, open the menu,
click New notebook, then Study and Learn."

"Write your project name and idea — for example: 'Marketing Exam Prep: I want to
revise the 4Ps of Marketing and Consumer Behaviour before my exam.'"

"Then upload your class notes or PPTs."

Prompt: "Help me study for my exams using my class notes and slides."

(wait for results) "See how much time this saves you."
```

**Watch for:** the moment they recognise their own slide deck being used. That's the story you post about.

---

## Demo 2 — Diagnostic Quiz

```
"Now the Diagnostic Quiz. Before your exams, run a quick quiz in the same
notebook to spot your syllabus gaps."

Ask: "Honestly — don't you think knowing your gaps first helps you revise better?"
(wait for their answer)

Show how it marks them strong in some units and weak in others — for example
strong in the 4Ps, weak in market segmentation.
```

---

## Demo 3 — Progress Dashboard, then close

```
"And the last one — the Progress Dashboard. After the lesson, it updates your
strengths and focus areas."

Show strengths (e.g. Product and Pricing) and top priorities (e.g. Segmentation
and Targeting).

Cue: "It tells you what to study next."  (wait for their response)

CLOSE: "So — do you think Study Notebooks would help you with your next exams?"
(wait)

"One last thing: please fill this post-survey form."
[share post-survey link — WAIT until they finish]

"Study Notebooks has way more to explore while you study. Good luck, and
thank you!"
```

---

## Do / don't

**Do**
- Show Study Notebooks live on your device, or share your screen
- Let them pick a topic from their own semester and try it
- Ask follow-ups when they look confused or curious
- Keep it a conversation
- Pick a quiet spot or a stable call

**Don't**
- Read the questions robotically
- Fill the forms for them — ever
- Rush it
- Interview students who already know Study Notebooks well
- Count an interview where one form is missing

## Scoring

- **15 points** per verified participant
- **10** minimum one-on-one sessions to unlock any points
- **50** maximum → **750 pts** ceiling
- Both forms complete = verified. One form = zero.

Log each interview in the portal right after it happens. Don't batch them for the 30th — verification problems take days to fix.
