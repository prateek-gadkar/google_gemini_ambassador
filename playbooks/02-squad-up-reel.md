## Shot list

| Time | Shot | Direction |
|------|------|-----------|
| **0–3s** | **HOOK** — face to camera | One line, flat delivery. No intro, no logo, no "hey guys". |
| 3–7s | The game + the fantasy | "Here's the game I'd pick, and the team I'd want." Props or a lobby screenshot. |
| 7–18s | **SCREEN RECORD** — Gemini | Type the prompt live. Let the roles and team name land on screen. |
| 18–25s | **POSTER REVEAL** — Nano Banana | Hold the beat. Say the team name out loud. |
| 25–30s | **CTA** | "Google Play Arena is open. Register now. Tag the teammate who's been AFK." |

Burn in captions — most people watch muted.


---

## Gemini prompt

```
Help me build my dream squad for [GAME NAME]. Suggest the ideal team roles and a cool team name.

Make it feel like a real roster: give each role a one-line playstyle, say why that person is on my team, and finish with a squad name I'd actually use and a one-line squad motto.
```

Don't clean up the output on screen. The raw answer is the proof.

---

## Nano Banana prompt

Text **is** allowed on this poster — the team name is the point.

```
Create a simple, bold squad poster for a gaming team.

Team name: [TEAM NAME]
Motto: [ONE LINE MOTTO]
Vibe: [neon esports / streetwear / retro arcade]

Show 4 to 5 character silhouettes or stylised portraits arranged like a team lineup,
each in the colour of their role. Big readable team name at the top.
Dark background, strong rim light, clean layout.

Keep it positive and campus-friendly. No weapons, no gore.
```
