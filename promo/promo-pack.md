# PART 3 — Rules to keep in mind while promoting

- **Never promise prizes.** Say "a chance at a Pixel" and "certificates for participants", exactly as the deck does. Overpromising is a code-of-conduct problem.
- **Don't invent statistics.** "180 million reach" is on the deck. Nothing else is.
- **Don't post the Meet link publicly** on an open story — send it in DMs or a closed group. Open links bring outsiders who aren't on your registration list, and a registration list that doesn't match the recording invalidates the event.
- **Don't share the certificates** as a promo hook ("get a free certificate!") — it reads as a certificate mill and undercuts the whole thing.
